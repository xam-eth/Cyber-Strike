# Gate 01 draft — Energy Core

**Asset ID:** `models__energy_core`
**Legacy source/export:** `source/aetherfall_kit.blend` → `assets/world/aetherfall/models/energy_core.glb`
**Legacy measured envelope:** 12.233 m X × 7.700 m Y × 10.647 m Z.
**Status:** draft brief; Base Mesh is **not authorized**.

## Role

The Energy Core supplies the landmark with a physically readable containment and service system. It is a separate construction assembly, not a cyan sphere/column dropped onto a plinth. The core must show how energy is contained, inspected, cooled, isolated, and connected to tower structure while retaining a safe protected void.

## Orthographic modelling guide

| Projection | Required read |
|---|---|
| **Front** | Centered protected core cavity is bracketed by asymmetrical containment frames and lower service plinth. Viewing/maintenance gaps must read as designed negative space, not missing geometry. |
| **Side** | Energy column/cage, heat/cooling manifolds, cable bus, inspection platform, and isolation panel establish a vertical service sequence. |
| **Top** | Radial or multi-sided containment ring is interrupted by an access bridge/service segment; major conduits have sources and destinations. |

## Functional Base-Mesh volumes

- Ground plinth and vibration/thermal isolation feet.
- Containment ring/cage with protected core volume.
- Access bridge or removable service segment.
- Intake/exhaust/coolant manifolds that visibly join a trunk.
- Power/data bus and lockout/isolation controls.
- Inspection platform/guard arrangement that expresses a believable maintenance workflow.

## Scale/camera checks

- Confirm relationship to tower foundation and actual route/cover use before accepting silhouette.
- Core cavity, rails, and platform clearance require the approved player/camera proxy.
- Review close interaction distance and distant landmark distance; emissive must not be used to compensate for an unclear containment silhouette.

## High-poly plan

Containment seam rings, precision bevels, insulated bus joints, cooling hoses/manifolds, clamp hardware, vent interiors, access latches, warning housings, heat staining, frosted cold junctions, and repairable panel interfaces. Energy effects remain later material/VFX work, never a replacement for the containment geometry.

## Material-breakdown plan

Dark painted structural containment, exposed alloy contact rings, high-temperature/cold-zone roughness variation, rubber/ceramic insulators, guarded glass/technical aperture as required, restricted cyan emissive confined to energy guidance and contained core volumes.

## Evidence required to accept Gate 01

Annotated front/side/top sheet, route/foundation placement review, approved proxy clearance capture, material-zone plan, and a high-poly feature list tied to bake intent.
