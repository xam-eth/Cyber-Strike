## Role

The Foundation anchors Archive/tower mass to the glacier basin, receives the route, distributes vertical load, contains lower service equipment, and establishes the transition between manufactured structure and ice/rock. It must not be a widened square pedestal with decorative ribs.

## Orthographic modelling guide

| Projection | Required read |
|---|---|
| **Approach/front** | Arrival apron steps into a low, broad central plinth. Major buttresses spread to ground with intentional openings/service bays between them. The route does not terminate at a decorative wall. |
| **Side** | Tower load resolves into sloped or stepped bearing blocks, anchor collars, drainage ledges, and an accessible lower service gallery. Ground contact is irregular because it responds to ice/terrain, not because random rocks were placed around it. |
| **Top** | Tower base footprint is nested inside a larger approach/service footprint. Access/service lanes, cable entries, and runoff routes create asymmetric function around a primarily axial landmark. |

## Functional Base-Mesh volumes

- Arrival/apron deck and route connection.
- Primary bearing plinth under tower/Archive load.
- Buttresses and load-transfer ribs that widen into terrain anchors.
- Lower service bays, inspection access, cable/coolant entry, and drain paths.
- Ice/rock interface shelves and anchor plates with room for physical frost/snow accumulation.

## Scale/camera checks

- Check approach width, side passage, cover/readability, and any stairs/ramps against an approved player proxy.
- At overview distance the base must prevent the landmark floating; at route distance it must provide real structural depth.
- Validate whether existing collision is intentionally retained, replaced, or reauthored. Visual mesh cannot silently dictate collision.

## High-poly plan

Bearing-plate breaks; deep footing seams; expansion joints; rock/ice anchor collars; drainage slots; worn steps; cable penetrations; service hatch/latch detail; compressed/frost-damaged terrain contact; bolt/weld patterns only at actual plates and connections.

## Material-breakdown plan

Painted structural mass, exposed load plates/anchor hardware, cold concrete/ceramic isolation where justified, rubberized expansion/drain interfaces, accumulated ice/snow in trap zones, and limited hazard/service markings.

## Evidence required to accept Gate 01

Annotated projections; approved proxy on approach/side route; foundation-to-terrain contact sheet; collision ownership decision; material and high-poly/bake plan.
