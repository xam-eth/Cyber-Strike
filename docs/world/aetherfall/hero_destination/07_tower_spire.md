# Gate 01 draft — Tower Spire

**Asset ID:** `models__tower_spire`
**Legacy source/export:** `source/aetherfall_kit.blend` → `assets/world/aetherfall/models/tower_spire.glb`
**Legacy measured envelope:** 9.916 m X × 18.500 m Y × 9.278 m Z.
**Status:** draft brief; Base Mesh is **not authorized**.

## Role

The Spire finishes the tower silhouette and carries sensing/communications/energy-receiver equipment. It is a skyline identifier, not an unstructured taper. Its height, crown hardware, and lower connection need a credible load/support/service story that reads both from the long camera and close to the tower.

## Orthographic modelling guide

| Projection | Required read |
|---|---|
| **Front** | Lower collar transitions to a tapering command/sensor shell. A distinct crown/halo/receiver breaks the tip silhouette; vertical fins or structural spines make the taper readable. |
| **Side** | At least one service climb/cable route and receiver support is visible. The narrow top remains structurally connected to the wider lower shell through ribs, not through a needle-like decorative join. |
| **Top** | Receiver orientation, cable/maintenance entry, and sensor array form a deliberate asymmetric arrangement that preserves strong 360-degree silhouette. |

## Functional Base-Mesh volumes

- Structural base collar and stack attachment.
- Tapered protected equipment shell.
- Exterior load fins/spines.
- Receiver/halo/sensor support with a protected service route.
- Crown equipment volume and lightning/thermal/communication path as appropriate.

## Scale/camera checks

- Review against frozen-spire/glacier skyline so it remains robot-built rather than becoming another ice shard.
- Test transition at the tier/spire joint and ensure the crown is visible from the main overview, bridge, catwalk, and lower recovery camera.
- Any ladders/platforms require approved proxy clearance; no implied climb gameplay is added without a design decision.

## High-poly plan

Crown sockets, receiver panels, dish/halo brackets, cable clamps, fin panel breaks, collar fasteners, access hatches, antenna/thermal hardware, wind/ice exposure wear, and seams that reflect removable equipment zones. Avoid uniformly dense micro-greeble on a skyline object.

## Material-breakdown plan

Dark coated shell, exposed alloy on receiver/collar interfaces, insulated cable bands, specialized dark sensor/radome surface if needed, controlled frost on upward/windward ledges, narrow cyan status/energy guidance only at actual receiver functions.

## Evidence required to accept Gate 01

Three-view turnaround in stack context; skyline comparison capture; identified support/service routing; approved scale proxy where access geometry exists; material and high-poly/bake plan.
