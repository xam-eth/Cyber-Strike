## Role

A Tower Tier is a repeatable but non-generic operational level between foundation and spire. It must communicate a distinct program: archive storage/service, cooling exchange, communications relay, or access transfer. Reuse cannot mean cloning blank cylinder/box geometry; the base kit needs defined join planes and controlled variant slots.

## Orthographic modelling guide

| Projection | Required read |
|---|---|
| **Front** | A central protected shell is split by a recessed operational belt and vertical fin/rib hierarchy. At least one access/service bay breaks symmetry with a reason. |
| **Side** | Upper/lower connection collars establish how this tier stacks. A maintenance balcony/vent bank/cable trunk is set behind guard or load frame, not pasted flat to the side. |
| **Top** | Defined stack interface, service-sector orientation, and offset receiver/cooling attachments. The tier needs an identifiable “front” so repeated instances can be rotated/varied intentionally. |

## Functional Base-Mesh volumes

- Lower and upper stack collars with actual mechanical transition.
- Protected core shell with recessed service belt.
- One function-specific service sector (vent/cable/access) that can accept controlled variants later.
- Load ribs/fins that join collar to shell.
- Maintenance or inspection recess with clearance and guard logic.

## Scale/camera checks

- Place tier within the tower stack at actual legacy world height before judging proportions.
- Verify repeated-tier seams cannot read as impossible gaps or z-fighting from overview and route cameras.
- Inspect the service sector at closest expected view; it must retain functional read rather than becoming random detail.

## High-poly plan

Stack gasket/bolt rings, panel/skin offsets, recessed belt hardware, service-door frames, vent depth, cable brackets, seam hierarchy, appropriate edge damage, and repeat-safe trim boundaries. Variant-specific details are planned as swappable modules, not baked random changes into every tier.

## Material-breakdown plan

Painted tier skins, exposed stack collars and bolt plates, darker cavity/vent interiors, insulated cable interfaces, controlled frost at upward-facing/seam traps, small service color/emissive markers only where operationally useful.

## Evidence required to accept Gate 01

Stack-context capture; annotated three-view guide with identified service sector; proxy/maintenance clearance check; controlled-variation plan; material and high-poly/bake list.
