# Gate 01 draft — Archive Computer

**Asset ID:** `archive__archive_computer`
**Legacy source/export:** `source/aetherfall_archive_interior.blend` → `assets/world/aetherfall/archive/archive_computer.glb`
**Legacy measured envelope:** 2.70 m X × 3.37 m Y × 2.52 m Z.
**Status:** draft brief; Base Mesh is **not authorized**.

## Role

This is the one named final-tech interface in the Archive room. It must read as an operable data-retrieval machine with a stable base, protected compute volume, operator interaction plane, service access, heat/cooling path, and visible relationship to the Data Core transaction. It is not a tall glowing box, a generic terminal, or a collection of unrelated cubes.

## Orthographic modelling guide

| Projection | Required silhouette/function |
|---|---|
| **Operator/front** | Human/camera-facing interaction plane sits under a protective hood. The screen/control surface is recessed, angled for viewing, and has readable hand/credential reach clearance. The base is wider and structurally grounded. |
| **Service side** | Distinct cooling intake/exhaust, removable service panel, cable/data trunk, and lockable cartridge/credential bay. The back is not a flat unfinished slab because the player can circle it. |
| **Top** | Hood, cooling chimney, cable entry, and status beacon form an asymmetric but balanced service hierarchy; no circular “sci-fi button” language without functional explanation. |

## Functional Base-Mesh volumes

| Volume | Purpose |

| Compute enclosure | Protected data-processing mass with panel hierarchy |

| Credential/Data-Core bay | Makes the stated progression transaction physically understandable |

## Scale/camera checks

- Position and turning clearance are tested with the approved player/camera proxy at the `archive_phase3.json` local computer position.
- Review at interaction distance, side-circulation distance, and the vestibule doorway composition. The machine cannot block the room or obscure the route.
- Animation, if retained, is reviewed as a mechanical sequence with clearance: no lid/panel moves through its own housing.

## High-poly plan

Bevel hierarchy around interaction hood; actual display recess; control detents; keyed credential/cartridge rails; cooling fin/baffle depth; removable panel seams; quarter-turn latches; cable gland; foot pads; fasteners at service joints; controlled wear at hand and maintenance contact zones.

## Material-breakdown plan

Painted dark shell; exposed conductive/alloy interfaces; dark anti-glare display/glass; rubberized controls/gaskets; small cyan/white status emissives; localized oily/polished handling wear. Every material needs a specific physical zone.

Front/side/top guide in Blender, approved interaction-height proxy, a mechanical animation/clearance note if animation remains, material assignment map, and named high-poly/bake feature list.
