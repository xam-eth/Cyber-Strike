# Combat HUD + shot feel: corrective design pass

Status: **direction provided by player; visual acceptance still OPEN.** The previous AEGIS control design is *not* accepted because a giant amber FIRE text disk, another text AIM disk and `EMP READY`/`SWAP · PULSE` pills resemble a prototype even though button events now work. This document is a design specification, not a claim of AAA parity.

## Evidence before drawing anything new

- Actual WebAssembly touch captures: `exports/qa-actions-touch/02-aim-held.png` cuts off the avatar at the left edge; `06-fire-held.png` loses the avatar/weapon almost entirely and shows a small pale-blue bolt (payload `b401a0a`). `exports/qa-defense-results-touch/04-fired.png` proves ammo 24→21 but **not** a compelling discharge (payload `50ff22c`). Both were taken from root at 390×844; gameplay camera/weapon assets did not change between these builds.
- Opt-in runtime telemetry in export `2d520a8` settled the framing cause: rest `aim=false, fov=73°, arm=6.09 m, camera z=14.01`; while FIRE `aim=false, fov=73°, arm=6.09 m, camera z=7.98`. There was **no ADS zoom or SpringArm collision**; `ThirdPersonCameraRig._apply_kick()` overwrote its child's `Camera3D.position.z`, teleporting the camera into the hero every shot. Remove that write; apply the small recoil push to the arm's *origin*, and add an in-engine regression assertion. ADS clipping is separate: portrait `aim spring=3.62 m, shoulder x=.64`; increase aim clearance before visual acceptance.
- Official Warframe Mobile Touch Control Guide: https://support.warframe.com/hc/en-us/articles/32633646081421-Warframe-Mobile-Touch-Control-Guide — dedicated *icon* for aim and shooting; press/hold to fire, hold to aim; left movement/right camera; abilities separate from weapon cycling and magazine; a sensor ring communicates off-frame hostiles. This is a shipped sci-fi game, not a theoretical mockup. Its documented auto-fire is **not** automatically adopted here: manual FIRE remains deliberate.
- Warframe's shipped iOS change: https://www.warframe.com/en/patch-notes/ios/38-5-0 — moves ADS to the right, retains movable touch HUD. The official old/new screenshot `481f73025d329313966b5c51d7b24bd2.jpg` shows grouped bright silhouette icons on low-noise dark controls without writing action names inside every button. Do not trace or copy those glyphs.
- Bungie, Destiny 2 weapon tuning (Sep 2025): https://www.bungie.net/7/en/News/Article/weapon_tuning_preview_ashiron — hip-fire reticle must indicate performance while reducing complexity and adapting to FOV/bloom/aim-assist; recoil should *not* hide the target. Apply to the *visual hierarchy*, not its copyrighted UI assets.

## Player-specified direction; implementation contract

1. **Immediate combat actions are icon-first.** FIRE = unmistakable authored muzzle/shot silhouette; ADS = optic sight; EMP = pulse/electric ring; WEAPON = weapon/rotate silhouette; PAUSE/RESUME = the standard glyph. No big `FIRE`, `AIM`, `EMP READY` or `SWAP · PULSE` strings *inside* those hit targets. Keep text in contextual Help/tooltips, ammo/weapon data in a separate read-only chip, and explicit `START DEFENSE`, `RETRY DEFENSE`, `RETURN TO EXPLORE`, `BUY/EQUIP` labels in high-consequence menus so their outcome is unambiguous.
2. **Thumb accuracy before style.** Portrait FIRE ≥112×112 physical pixels, ADS/EMP ≥72×72, WEAPON ≥60 high, PAUSE ≥56; landscape FIRE ≥104×104, ADS/EMP ≥64×64, WEAPON ≥60 high. Preserve ≥6 px physical gap between separate right-thumb hit rects, safe-area spacing, handedness and the existing touch editor. Differentiate silhouette at 390×844 *without reading captions*. Use transparent low-noise dark bodies with light icons and state-specific amber/cyan arcs; a saturated amber disk must not dominate the scene at rest.
3. **Shot has a visible beginning, journey and result:** the Blender rig raises/recovers the rifle; measured MuzzlePoint drives a directional flash, a moving tracer visibly connected to that point, and impact sparks at real ray collision. Reticle tightens on ADS and confirms real hits. A restrained **32-px physical touch-only soft lock** may correct the muzzle direction for a nearby visible Runner while FIRE/ADS is *manually* held, after a line-of-sight check; cyan reticle previews the assist, amber still requires a real hit. No auto-fire, no aim-through-cover, no desktop magnetism. Shot sound remains synchronous; reduce peripheral screen kick so target stays readable. Inspect screenshot *while* FIRE is held and with/without ADS, in portrait and landscape, not just ammo after release. Preserve projectile/damage physics and accessibility reductions.
4. **No hidden enemy ambush in Explore.** Defense stays explicit; once the player starts it, directional hostile information (not another text banner across the sightline) helps locate enemies outside view. Reward +10 BP on real Runner KO; +100 on cleared wave. Results→Return→Armory→purchase/equip→replay requires separate browser QA; a zero-BP cash-out and a unit test are not acceptance for this loop.
5. **Screenshots and observability:** compare old/new root and `/web/index.html` at 390×844 and 844×390; screenshot FIRE press, ADS hold, EMP cooldown, Defense, Results, Armory. Verify touch events, visual geometry and hit feedback separately; ensure no Godot runtime/script errors. A human visual review is still required for artistic acceptance; passing CI alone is not it.

## What this specifically rejects

- Simply enlarging label text, pasting an icon in front of the same large words, or repainting the whole yellow disk.
- Cosmetic muzzle particles not attached to the measured rifle barrel or projectile hits that do not reflect gameplay.
- Headless button-signal tests as proof that physical fingers can play it, or a short GIF/single frame as proof animation reads under motion.
- Importing a copyrighted Warframe/Destiny icon sheet. Only original SVG glyphs and already-licensed local resources.
